What I do (In simple words)

What are LLMs and AI Agents? LLMs (Large Language Models like ChatGPT) are 'brains' that understand and generate text. AI Agents are these brains put on autopilot. An agent can read an instruction, make decisions, use tools, and execute tasks automatically — without you doing it manually every day.

How I can help your business: I build these agents to automate your routine. I can set up Telegram or Instagram bots for your customers, integrate payment systems (like YooKassa) so they buy directly in the chat, and automate the delivery of your services. I can also set up secure remote servers or teach you or your team how to use AI tools to save hours of work every day.
